Chemical Kinetics - Class 12 Chemistry

1. परिचय (Introduction)

Chemical Kinetics वह शाखा है जो रासायनिक प्रतिक्रियाओं की गति, rate और factors affecting rate का अध्ययन करती है।

2. Rate of Reaction (प्रतिक्रिया की दर)

Rate = change in concentration of reactant or product per unit time

Rate = - (Δ[A]/Δt) = (Δ[B]/Δt)

  • A → B
  • Δ[A] = Reactant concentration change
  • Δt = Time interval

3. Factors Affecting Rate of Reaction

  • Concentration of reactants
  • Temperature
  • Pressure (for gases)
  • Nature of reactants
  • Catalyst presence
  • Surface area (for solids)

4. Rate Law & Order of Reaction

Rate law: Rate = k [A]^m [B]^n

  • k = rate constant
  • m,n = order of reaction w.r.t A & B
  • Total order = m+n

Integrated rate laws:

  • First order: ln[A] = ln[A]₀ - kt
  • Second order: 1/[A] = 1/[A]₀ + kt
  • Zero order: [A] = [A]₀ - kt

5. Half-life (t½)

  • First order: t½ = 0.693/k
  • Second order: t½ = 1/(k[A]₀)
  • Zero order: t½ = [A]₀/(2k)

6. Arrhenius Equation

Temperature effect on rate constant:

k = A e^(-Ea/RT)

  • k = rate constant
  • A = frequency factor
  • Ea = activation energy (J/mol)
  • R = gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K)
  • T = temperature in K

Logarithmic form: log k = log A - Ea/(2.303RT)

7. Reaction Mechanism & Molecularity

  • Molecularity: Number of reactant molecules in an elementary step (unimolecular, bimolecular, termolecular)
  • Mechanism: Sequence of elementary steps leading to overall reaction
  • Rate-determining step controls overall rate

8. Energy Profile Diagram (SVG)

Activation energy (Ea) represents the energy barrier that reactants must overcome to form products.

  • Rate = k[A]^n
